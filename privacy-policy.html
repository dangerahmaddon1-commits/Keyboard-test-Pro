Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Keyboard Test Pro handles information when you use https://keyboardtest-pro.vercel.app/.

Keyboard test data

The keyboard tester processes key events in your browser to show which keys were detected. It does not need to transmit your keystrokes to our server to provide the test. For your privacy, do not type passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information while testing.

Analytics

This website may use Vercel Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and improve reliability. Analytics may process technical usage information under the provider's own policies.

Advertising

The site includes the Google AdSense loader while the site is being prepared for review. Ad placements are not activated by this page. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their applicable policies and consent settings.

Cookies and third parties

Third-party services, if enabled, may use their own technologies and policies. Please review the privacy policies of those providers directly.

Children and changes

This tool is intended as a general utility and does not knowingly request personal information from children. We may update this policy when the site or applicable requirements change.
